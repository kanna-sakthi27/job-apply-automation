## §1 — Channel-specific mechanics

- Jaabz is an aggregator: it hands off to either **LinkedIn Easy Apply** or the employer's ATS.
  Follow the destination's own playbook rather than inventing a third flow.
- Listings are visa-sponsorship tagged by default, which makes this the highest-yield channel
  when sponsorship is required — but the tag is a signal, not a guarantee. Verify against the
  posting text before counting it as in-scope.
- Count an application as applied **only** on the confirmation state, then flip the job's
  "Mark Applied" toggle and verify it turns active.
